Lemon Man

Woah what a cool leman guy!

Stats

  1. Type: Lemon
  2. Health: 10pts
  3. Attack: 5pts
  4. Defence: 100pts

Moves

  • Good Job

    Lemon Man cheers on friendly creature. Loses 10pts of defence and adds it to friendly creature's defence.

  • Lemon Time

    Lemon Man gets super angry and explodes. Loses all health pts and deals 15pts of damage to target.

  • Poke

    Lemon pokes the eyes of target creature and causes blind for 1 turn and deals damage based on attack.

  • Dehydrated Yellow

    Lemon Man notices your dehydrated, shoot lemon juice at target and deals damage based on attack.
